Bank Customer Churn Analysis – Power BI
Project Overview
This project analyzes customer churn behavior in a retail banking environment using Power BI. The objective is to identify key factors influencing customer attrition and provide actionable insights to support customer retention strategies.
The dashboard explores customer demographics, product ownership patterns, geographic distribution, and financial risk indicators associated with churn.

**Business Problem**
Customer churn directly impacts profitability and long-term business growth. Understanding why customers leave and identifying high-risk segments enables organizations to implement targeted retention initiatives and improve customer satisfaction.
This analysis was conducted to answer the following business questions:
•	Which customer segments are most likely to churn?
•	Which countries exhibit the highest churn levels?
•	How do age and gender influence customer attrition?
•	Does product ownership impact churn behavior?
•	Which customers represent the highest financial risk to the bank?

**Dashboard Structure**
**Dashboard 1 – Executive Summary**
Provides a high-level overview of customer churn performance through key performance indicators and trend analysis.
Key Metrics:
•	Total Customers
•	Churned Customers
•	Churn Rate
•	Average Balance
•	Average Credit Score
Business Value:
Provides management with an executive-level view of customer retention performance.

**Dashboard 2 – Customer Demographics & Churn Analysis**
Analyzes customer behavior across demographic segments.
Visualizations:
•	Churn Customers by Country
•	Customer Distribution by Country
•	Churn by Age Group
•	Gender Distribution of Churned Customers
Business Insights:
•	Certain countries demonstrate significantly higher churn rates than others.
•	Middle-aged customer segments contribute a substantial portion of churn.
•	Gender-based analysis reveals differences in customer retention patterns.
Business Value:
Helps identify demographic segments requiring focused retention strategies.

**Dashboard 3 – Product Ownership Analysis**
Examines the relationship between banking product adoption and customer churn.
Visualizations:
•	Churn by Number of Products
•	Product Ownership Distribution
•	Product Usage Segmentation
Business Insights:
•	Customers with specific product ownership patterns display elevated churn risk.
•	Product engagement levels influence customer loyalty and retention.
Business Value:
Supports cross-selling and customer engagement initiatives.

**Dashboard 4 – Risk Analysis**
Evaluates financial risk indicators associated with customer churn.
Visualizations:
•	Risk Segment Distribution
•	Balance vs Salary Risk Matrix
•	Risk Category Analysis
•	Financial Customer Segmentation
Risk Categories:
•	High Risk
•	Medium Risk
•	Low Risk

**Business Insights:**
•	High-balance inactive customers represent a critical retention risk.
•	Financial indicators provide strong signals for churn prediction.
•	Risk segmentation enables prioritization of retention efforts.
Business Value:
Supports proactive customer retention and risk management strategies.

**Key Analytical Findings**
1.	Customer churn varies significantly across countries.
2.	Age is a major factor influencing customer attrition.
3.	Product ownership behavior impacts customer retention.
4.	High-balance inactive customers represent the highest financial risk.
5.	Risk segmentation helps prioritize retention strategies and resource allocation.
